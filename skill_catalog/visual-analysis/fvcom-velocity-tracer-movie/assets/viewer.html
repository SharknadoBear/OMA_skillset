Reading the current field

Snapshot tails depict an accelerated flow through one fixed field. Continuous mode interpolates the changing field on its model clock. Visual speed independently multiplies particle travel: 1× uses the model velocity, while other values are illustrative motion. Playback duration controls the field clock. These particles are not validated Lagrangian trajectories.

Trail duration is the maximum age in active playback seconds. Trails fade smoothly to zero and expire completely. Pause freezes their age. Colors and hover velocities always show the unscaled field.

Water shading and motion use area-weighted connected wet neighborhoods and barycentric interpolation on the native triangles. Native element values remain available in the hover readout. Interpolated speed is calculated from interpolated components.

Map edges are reconstructed from the model mesh. Solid boundaries are pale; supplied open boundary edges are dashed. Pan by dragging and zoom with the wheel or buttons.
